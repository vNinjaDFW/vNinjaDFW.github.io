As enterprises increasingly adopt Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) for its performance, scalability, and cost efficiency, ensuring **data protection and cyber-resilience** has become more critical than ever.

In 2024, **61% of organizations** experienced cloud breaches—nearly **3x** the number from the year before. This rapid growth in threats highlights the urgent need for cloud-native solutions that can safeguard data across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

## 🚀 Introducing Rubrik for OCI

Rubrik now extends its Security Cloud to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, offering **native support for backup and recovery** of:

- Oracle databases running on OCI virtual machines
- Oracle Cloud VMware Solution (OCVS)

This ensures secure, policy-based, and scalable data protection—without the operational overhead.

## 🔐 Key Capabilities

### ✅ Centralized Management
Rubrik delivers **unified visibility and control** over OCI workloads across accounts and regions, eliminating the need for manual scripting or siloed tools.

### 🔁 Seamless Integration
Backups are application-consistent via:
- **Oracle RMAN** integration for database protection
- **VMware APIs** for agentless, consistent VM-level backups in OCVS

### 📅 SLA-Driven Protection
Rubrik automates protection by detecting and applying SLAs to new workloads—**no manual intervention** needed.

### 💾 Efficient Backup & Storage
- **Incremental-forever backups** reduce impact and bandwidth usage
- **Global deduplication** ensures storage optimization in OCI Object Storage

### ⚡ Fast Recovery
- **Point-in-time restores** for Oracle databases
- **Live Mount & Instant Cloning**
- **Near-instant recovery** of OCVS virtual machines

### 🛡️ Enterprise-Grade Security
Rubrik’s OCI solution includes:
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- End-to-end encryption (in transit & at rest)
- Immutable backups & audit logging

## 🌐 Why It Matters

This solution is ideal for customers looking to:

- **Standardize protection** across on-prem, hybrid, and cloud workloads
- **Accelerate recovery** from ransomware or corruption events
- **Reduce costs** with efficient backup and tiered OCI storage
- **Enhance compliance and reporting** with built-in governance tools

## ✅ Final Thoughts

Rubrik’s OCI-native backup and recovery capabilities provide the **automation, efficiency, and security** modern enterprises need. Whether you're migrating legacy workloads or managing a large-scale Oracle environment, Rubrik helps ensure your data is **resilient, recoverable, and always protected**.
